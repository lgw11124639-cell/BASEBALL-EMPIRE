# 2.0.43 · Accurate training reports and cumulative ball specifications

Training changes are now recorded directly from the actual player's ID and dated metadata. Previously, the event builder searched again by name and could select a retired namesake. Club training now excludes inactive registrations even when a legacy retirement flag is missing. Monthly notices preserve structured player summaries with verified identities and dated registration/position/age. Growth/decline totals are event counts (건), distinct verified players are counted separately.

Existing monthly body text is read without rewriting history. Known formats render as an aligned table and three summary cards; ambiguous legacy names are not linked to a guessed profile or presented as verified status. Original text remains available. The structured monthly format no longer receives the false “player details not retained” message. General notice/report dialogs are full-screen, with underlying page scroll locked and restored on close; profile closing returns to the report. Old inset-window geometry was removed from the canonical stylesheet.

Ball policies now accept inspected physical specifications as well as legacy preset IDs. The automatic low/high batting-average review adjusts the current rebound index by +0.010/-0.010, rather than stopping after selecting one of three preset IDs. Successive seasons can therefore adopt 1.010, 1.020, 1.030, etc. Physical bounds are 0.800–1.300; at a boundary no further outward proposal is generated. A minimum 100 completed league games and the existing .235/.300 thresholds remain. Consultation, vote, inspection, next-season application, current-season immutability and international isolation are retained. Custom specifications persist across unchanged seasons and are used for new annual inspections. The office labels the three products as reference specifications and displays cumulative-adjustment rules. Minimum-salary policy behavior is unchanged.
