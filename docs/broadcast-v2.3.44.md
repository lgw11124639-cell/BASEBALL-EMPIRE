# v2.3.44 — pitcher glove anatomy, ready-size proportions, hitter-sized zone, readable field names

The incorrect hand-separation pose (frame 2) is replaced for all 10 club and 28 national uniforms. Each replacement is a complete generated figure: the left glove folds inward against the chest, mostly occluded by the torso, and the separate right hand holds the baseball near the waist. No glove or limb patches are composited over another pose. The same early hand-separation pose is used for each delivery; arm-slot differences continue in later authored frames. Earlier rejected outward-facing glove drafts are not shipped.

The generated wardrobe boards have variable cell widths. Packaging detects their actual grid borders, removes border pixels, extracts the entire figure, and registers its supporting shoe before replacing the entire atlas cell. All 1064 pose cells are checked for clean corners, complete bodies, and planted shoes. Updated source hashes identify the replacement wardrobe board and cell.

Moving-pose bulk is calibrated to each uniform's standing-ready silhouette rather than allowing the initial lift to inflate the body. One scale factor is held across the moving frames of each delivery. Horizontal and vertical factors preserve the reference silhouette area while giving a slimmer, taller projection; the complete figure is 7% taller vertically at ready. Planted shoes and the release coordinate use their corresponding axis factors. Release coordinates are placed inside the authored throwing-hand silhouette so downscaling does not place the ball on a barely transparent edge pixel.

The strike zone is projected from the batter's normal ready stance: shoulder and knee landmarks at y=96 and y=264 in the authored 384-pixel cell, registered with the actual shoe baseline and body scale. Zone width is narrowed to 58–78 logical pixels, instead of the previous fixed 108. It remains steady through swings and uses the normal ready reference during bunts. The plate, batting-box shoe registration and recorded post-break pitch endpoints remain unchanged.

Runner and fielder names are rendered in one shared SVG label layer. Labels follow actual moving player points, reserve space for other names, player dots, verdict panels, and error marks, and use leader lines when moved farther from the player. Full foreign names remain available. The former name text stays in the player node for existing labels/metadata but is visually hidden. Labels do not change replay routes or game results.
